YOUR DESKTOP, REMIXED

A fresh look in a few clicks.

  1. Choose your colours. Start with a mood, then click any palette colour to change it.
  2. Add your own (optional). Tap the + tile to upload any SVG. Its colours are matched to your palette, and it stays in this browser.
  3. Download your pack. Select your operating system. Unzip the download to find your icons and installation guide.
  4. Apply your icons. Your download changes the icon artwork; apply each icon in your operating system.

Windows: Right-click a shortcut → Properties → Shortcut → Change Icon, then choose its ICO file.

macOS: Select a file or folder → Get Info, then drag its ICNS file onto the small icon at the top. Some protected apps cannot be changed.

Linux: Use your desktop environment’s launcher or folder properties to select a PNG or SVG. Steps vary by desktop.

App updates may reset custom icons. These are original decorative icons, not official app logos.
